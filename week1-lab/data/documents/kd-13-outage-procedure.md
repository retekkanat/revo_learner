# Outage procedure for support staff

Last reviewed: 2026-09-28
Document id: kd_13
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: internal
Status: current

This procedure tells support staff what to do when network operations declares an outage. An outage is declared when a single network failure affects many customers in one postcode area. Each declared outage has an incident reference that starts with inc_, and the internal status board shows its area and its estimated restoration time.

## When a customer reports no service

1. Check the status board for the customer's postcode area before you run any fault checks.
2. If the area has a declared outage, link the case to the incident reference.
3. Don't book an engineer visit. The incident team assigns engineers to the outage, and a home visit can't fix it.
4. Give the customer the estimated restoration time shown on the status board. Never give your own estimate or a time from any other source.

## While the outage continues

Update every linked case at least every 4 hours while the incident is open, even if nothing has changed. Each update gives the current estimated restoration time from the status board. If the estimate changes, update every linked case straight away.

## When the outage ends

When network operations closes the incident, ask each customer to restart their router. If the service still doesn't work after a restart, treat it as a new fault with the normal repair target.

## Compensation

A declared outage doesn't stop automatic compensation. Count each customer's repair target from the time they reported the problem, as described in Service levels and compensation. Business 500 customers receive service credits instead.
