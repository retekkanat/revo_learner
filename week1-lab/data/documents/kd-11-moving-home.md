# Moving home

Last reviewed: 2026-03-30
Document id: kd_11
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

You can take your Kestrel broadband with you when you move. Tell us at least 14 days before your moving date, either online at www.kestrel.example/help or by calling customer care on 01632 960100.

## If your plan is available at your new address

We check whether your plan is available at your new address. If it is, your plan moves with you at no charge. Your monthly price stays the same, and your minimum term continues unchanged. If your new home doesn't have a Kestrel fibre box, we'll book an engineer visit to fit one, as described in Engineer visits.

## If your plan isn't available

If your current plan isn't available but another Kestrel plan is, you can choose that plan instead. If the new plan costs more each month, a new 18-month minimum term starts. If it costs less, your minimum term continues unchanged.

If Kestrel can't provide any plan at your new address, you can cancel without paying an early termination charge. The usual 30-day notice period still applies, so tell us as early as you can.

## On moving day

Take your KR-3 router with you. Set it up at your new home in the same way as before, using the guide Setting up the KR-3 router.

Your billing date stays the same after the move.
