# Cancelling your plan

Last reviewed: 2026-08-20
Document id: kd_12
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

You can cancel your Kestrel plan at any time by giving 30 days' notice. Call customer care on 01632 960100, or use the cancellation form at www.kestrel.example/help. Your service ends 30 days after the day you give notice.

## Early termination charge

If you cancel before your minimum term ends, you pay an early termination charge. We work it out by multiplying your monthly price by the number of whole months left in your minimum term, and then taking off 20 per cent.

For example, if you're on Fibre 300 at £34.00 a month and 6 months of your minimum term are left, the charge is £34.00 × 6 = £204.00, less 20 per cent, which is £163.20.

You don't pay an early termination charge in these cases:

- You cancel within the 14-day cooling-off period.
- We haven't restored your minimum guaranteed speed within 30 days, as described in Speed guarantee.
- We can't provide any plan at your new address when you move home.
- We raise your price in a way that isn't set out in your contract.

## Returning your router

When your service ends, we send you a prepaid bag for your KR-3 router. Return the router within 30 days of your service ending. If we don't receive it, we charge £50.00.

## Your final bill

We send your final bill after your service ends. It includes any part-month charge, any early termination charge and any credits on your account. If we owe you money, we refund it as described in Refund rules.
