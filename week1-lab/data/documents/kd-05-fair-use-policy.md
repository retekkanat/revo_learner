# Fair use policy

Last reviewed: 2026-06-10
Document id: kd_05
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

Every Kestrel plan includes unlimited data. Kestrel doesn't cap how much you download, and it doesn't slow, prioritise or manage your traffic at any time of day because of how much you use. Kestrel stopped managing traffic on its network in 2024, when the plans it sold before then were retired.

The fair use policy covers a small number of uses that can harm other customers or the network.

- Home plans are for use by the people in your household. You mustn't resell your connection or share it with neighbouring properties.
- You mustn't use a home plan to run a service for the public, such as a commercial web hosting service. Business 500 is designed for that kind of use.
- You mustn't use your connection to send unsolicited bulk email or to attack other networks.

Sometimes a device in your home is infected with malicious software and sends large amounts of harmful traffic without your knowledge. If we detect this, we'll contact you by email and text message and explain what we've found. If the traffic continues and harms the network, we may block that traffic only, after telling you. We won't slow the rest of your connection.

If you break this policy repeatedly after we've contacted you, we may end your contract. We'll always contact you first and explain why.

If you have a question about this policy, contact technical support on 01632 960150.
