# Speed guarantee

Last reviewed: 2026-06-22
Document id: kd_04
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

Every Kestrel plan has a minimum guaranteed download speed. If your speed falls below it and we can't fix the problem, you can leave your plan without paying an early termination charge.

| Plan | Minimum guaranteed download speed |
| --- | --- |
| Essential 80 | 40 Mbps |
| Fibre 300 | 150 Mbps |
| Fibre 900 | 450 Mbps |
| Business 500 | 300 Mbps |

## Check your speed

Wi-Fi speeds don't count towards the guarantee, because walls, distance and other devices affect them. To check your speed:

1. Connect a computer to the router with a network cable.
2. Close other programs, and disconnect other devices if you can.
3. Run the Kestrel speed test at www.kestrel.example/speed.
4. Repeat the test on three different days.

## Claim under the guarantee

If the average of your three wired tests is below your plan's minimum guaranteed download speed, contact technical support on 01632 960150 and give us your results. We'll run a line test and investigate.

We have 30 calendar days from the day you first reported the problem to restore your minimum guaranteed speed. If we haven't restored it by then, you can leave your plan without an early termination charge, or move to a slower plan at that plan's monthly price without starting a new minimum term.

We'll keep you updated while we investigate, and we'll tell you the date on which the 30 days end.
