# Price changes

Owner team: billing
Audience: customer
Status: current

Your contract sets out how your monthly price changes each year. This page explains the yearly change and what happens if Kestrel needs to make any other change.

## The yearly price change

On 1 April each year, your monthly price rises by a fixed amount:

| Plan type | Yearly rise |
| --- | --- |
| Home plans | £3.00 a month |
| Business 500 | £4.00 a month, excluding VAT |

For example, if you're on Essential 80 at £26.00 a month, your price becomes £29.00 a month from 1 April. We remind you of the change on your bill at least 30 days before it happens.

Because the yearly change is set out in your contract, it doesn't give you a right to cancel without an early termination charge.

## Other price changes

If Kestrel needs to change your price in any other way, we tell you at least 30 days before the change. You can then cancel your plan without an early termination charge, as long as you tell us within 30 days of our notice.

## Prices for new customers

The prices on our plan pages are for new customers and for customers who move to a new plan. Your own price is shown on your latest bill.
