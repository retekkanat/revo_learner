# Week 1 Friday review

The Friday review is a five-minute conversation with your instructor about your progress in Week 1. You discuss your knowledge check results and exercises, then agree what to study next. Your instructor records the discussion. The review isn't assessed, and you don't need to submit work.

## Review timetable

On Day 5, the instructor demonstrates the complete Support Assistant v1 to the class in Block 3. The reviews then take place in Block 4, from 15:15 to 16:15, while the class studies the second half of AWS Partner: Agentic AI Technical Foundations. The instructor sees each learner in turn for five minutes. After the reviews, the instructor starts the teardown of the week's demonstration resources and sets the weekend reading. The Day 5 knowledge check follows at 16:25, and the day closes when the instructor shows that the resources have been removed.

## Prepare for the review

Bring these three things:

- Your knowledge check results for Days 1 to 4, with the number of correct answers each day.
- A list of the exercises you did and your progress on each. You can also show any results you saved.
- The subjects that you found hardest this week.

## Review discussion

1. You give your knowledge check results for Days 1 to 4.
2. You say which exercises you did and how far you got. If you want to, you show the results that you saved.
3. You and the instructor agree what to study next, such as a handbook section, a Skill Builder lesson, an exam guide task statement or a Week 2 exercise.
4. The instructor asks whether you can sign in to your AWS account yet. You can complete an exercise's functions and run its check without an account, but the workbook cells that call AWS need your own account. You arrange it through your employer or a personal account by the start of Week 2, as the course's account requirement describes.

After the Day 5 knowledge check, tell the instructor how many Day 5 questions you answered correctly, and the instructor adds the result to your record.
