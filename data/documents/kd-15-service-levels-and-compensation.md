# Service levels and compensation

Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

This page sets out how quickly Kestrel fixes faults and what we pay automatically when we don't meet our service levels. Working days are Monday to Friday, except public holidays.

## Repair targets

| Customer | Repair target |
| --- | --- |
| Home plans | The end of the second working day after you report the fault |
| Priority support customers | The end of the next calendar day after you report the fault |
| Business 500 | The end of the next working day after you report the fault |

## Automatic compensation for home plans

You don't need to claim this compensation. We credit it to your bill within 30 days.

- **Repair delay:** If we don't fix a fault by the repair target, we credit £8.00 for each calendar day after the target until your service is restored.
- **Missed engineer appointment:** If our engineer misses a booked appointment, we credit £25.00 to your account automatically.
- **Delayed activation:** If your new service doesn't start on the activation date we agreed, we credit £5.00 for each calendar day until it starts.

Business 500 customers receive service credits instead, as described on the Business 500 page.

## When compensation doesn't apply

We don't pay compensation in these cases:

- The fault was caused by your own equipment, or by damage inside your home.
- You asked for an engineer appointment later than the first one we offered. In that case, the days between the two appointments don't count.

A declared outage doesn't stop compensation. Each customer's repair target starts from the time they reported the problem.

Goodwill credits are separate from automatic compensation. They're decided case by case.
