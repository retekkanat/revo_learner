# Digital voice home phone

Last reviewed: 2026-03-09
Document id: kd_23
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

Every Kestrel home plan includes a digital voice home phone service. Your calls travel over your broadband connection instead of a separate copper phone line.

## Setting up your phone

Plug your phone into the socket labelled Phone on the back of the KR-3 router. You don't need to plug anything into a wall socket. If your phone has a separate base station, plug the base station into the router.

## Calls

Calls are charged as you use them and appear on your bill. Calls to the emergency services are always free.

## Power cuts

Your router needs mains power, so your home phone stops working during a power cut unless you have a battery backup unit. A battery backup unit keeps the digital voice service working for at least an hour.

The battery backup unit is free if you're registered for priority support, or if you have no mobile phone signal at home. Other customers can buy one for £45.00 from customer care on 01632 960100.

If you don't have a battery backup unit, use a mobile phone to call for help during a power cut.

## Phone faults

If your phone has no dial tone, first check that it's plugged into the Phone socket on the router and that the router light is solid white. Try another handset if you have one. If the problem continues, report a fault to technical support on 01632 960150. Phone faults have the same repair targets as broadband faults.
