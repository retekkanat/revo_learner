# Priority support

Last reviewed: 2026-04-27
Document id: kd_22
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

Priority support is for customers who rely on their broadband or home phone because of a health condition, a disability or their age. It's free, and you can register at any time.

## What priority support gives you

- A faster repair target: we fix faults by the end of the next calendar day after you report them, including at weekends.
- A free battery backup unit, which keeps the digital voice home phone working for at least an hour during a power cut.
- The earliest available engineer appointments when a visit is needed.
- The option to name a trusted person whom we can contact about faults if we can't reach you.

## How to register

Call customer care on 01632 960100, or use the form at www.kestrel.example/help. You can register for yourself or for someone in your household. We'll ask a few questions about why you need priority support so that we can help in the right way. You don't need to send any medical evidence.

## How we use your information

We use the information you give us only to provide priority support. We record that you're registered, but we don't record more detail about your health than we need. You can ask us to remove your registration at any time.

## If your needs change

Tell us if your needs change, for example if you no longer need the battery backup unit or if you want to change your trusted person.
