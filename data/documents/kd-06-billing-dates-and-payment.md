# Billing dates and payment

Kestrel bills monthly in advance. This page explains when your bill is issued, what your first bill covers and when we collect payment.

## Your billing date

Kestrel issues bills on four dates each month: the 1st, the 8th, the 15th and the 22nd. Your billing date is the first of these dates after your service starts. Each bill covers the month that starts on that billing date.

## Your first bill

Your first bill is usually higher than your monthly price. It includes a part-month charge for the days from your activation date up to the day before your first billing date, plus the next full month in advance. We work out the part-month charge by dividing your monthly price by 30 and multiplying the result by the number of days.

For example, if your Fibre 300 service starts on the 5th and your billing date is the 8th, your first bill includes three days at £1.13 a day, which is £3.40, plus £34.00 for the month from the 8th. The total is £37.40.

## Paying your bill

We collect payment by direct debit 10 days after the bill date. You can also pay by debit or credit card online, or by calling billing on 01632 960170.

Online bills are free and appear in your online account. Paper bills cost £1.50 a month.

## Changing your billing date

You can move your billing date to another of the four dates once in any 12 months. Your next bill then includes a one-off part-month charge for the extra days.
