If your broadband or home phone stops working, these checks often fix the problem quickly. If they don't, report a fault and we'll take it from there.

## Before you report a fault

1. Check the service status page at status.kestrel.example. If there's a declared outage in your area, you don't need to report it. The page shows the estimated restoration time.
2. Look at the light on your router and check what it means in Setting up the KR-3 router.
3. Restart the router: switch it off at the back, wait 30 seconds and switch it on again.
4. If you can, connect a computer to the router with a network cable to find out whether the problem is the line or the Wi-Fi.

## How to report a fault

Call technical support on 01632 960150 at any time, or report the fault online at www.kestrel.example/help. Tell us what you've already tried.

## What happens next

We run a remote line test, usually within 1 hour of your report. If the test finds a fault on the Kestrel network, we fix it within the repair target for your plan, which starts from the time you reported the fault. Service levels and compensation explains the repair targets and what we pay if we miss them.

If the fault needs an engineer, we'll book a visit with you. If the engineer finds that your own equipment or wiring caused the fault, we may charge for the visit, as described in Engineer visits.

We'll update your case when the fault is fixed and ask you to confirm that everything works.
