# New connections and activation

Last reviewed: 2026-07-15
Document id: kd_20
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

When you order a Kestrel plan, we give you an activation date. That's the day your service starts.

## How long it takes

Most new connections start within 10 working days of your order. If your home already has a Kestrel fibre box, we can often start sooner. If your home needs a fibre box, an engineer visits to fit it, and we book the visit with you when you order, as described in Engineer visits.

## Before your activation date

We deliver your KR-3 router at least 2 working days before your activation date. Keep it in its box until your activation day.

## On your activation day

We switch your service on by 1pm. Set up your router after that, using the guide Setting up the KR-3 router. Your first bill is issued on the first billing date after your activation date, as described in Billing dates and payment.

## If your activation is delayed

If your service doesn't start on the activation date we agreed, we credit compensation to your bill automatically, as described in Service levels and compensation. You don't need to claim it. We'll also tell you the new activation date as soon as we know it.

## Changing or cancelling your order

You can change your plan or your activation date free of charge at any time before your activation day. You can cancel your order within 14 days of placing it without any charge, as described in Refund rules.
