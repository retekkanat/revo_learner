# Contacting Kestrel support

Last reviewed: 2026-09-15
Document id: kd_01
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

Kestrel has three support teams. Each team has its own phone line and opening hours, so that you reach the right people the first time you call.

| Team | What it handles | Phone | Opening hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer care | Orders, moving home, cancellations, complaints and general questions | 01632 960100 | 8am to 8pm, Monday to Saturday |
| Technical support | Faults, slow speeds, routers, engineer visits and outages | 01632 960150 | 24 hours a day, every day |
| Billing | Bills, payments, refunds and credits | 01632 960170 | 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday |

Business 500 customers have their own business support line on 01632 960200, which is open from 7am to 9pm every day.

You can also email help@kestrel.example at any time. We reply to emails within 2 working days. A working day is Monday to Friday, except public holidays.

The Kestrel support assistant answers questions online at any time at www.kestrel.example/help. It answers from Kestrel's published support documents, and it can look up the cases that belong to your account once you have signed in. It can't change your account, apply credits or take payments. When it can't answer a question, it tells you so and suggests the team to contact.

Before you report a fault, check the service status page at status.kestrel.example. It shows any outage that Kestrel has declared in your area and the estimated time that service will be restored.

When you contact us, have your customer reference ready. You can find it at the top of every bill. We'll ask you some security questions before we discuss your account, as described in Account security and privacy.
