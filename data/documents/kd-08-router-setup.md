# Setting up the KR-3 router

Last reviewed: 2026-04-14
Document id: kd_08
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

The KR-3 router comes with a power supply, a network cable and a card that shows your Wi-Fi name and password. Set it up on your activation day, after 1pm.

## Connect the router

1. Find the fibre box that the engineer fitted on the wall. It has a port labelled LAN 1.
2. Connect the network cable from LAN 1 on the fibre box to the red WAN port on the back of the router.
3. Connect the power supply to the router and switch it on.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the light on the front of the router to turn solid white.
5. Connect your devices to the Wi-Fi network shown on the card, using the password on the same card.

You can change the Wi-Fi name and password in the Kestrel app.

## What the router light means

| Light | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid white | Connected | Nothing. Your service is working. |
| Flashing white | Starting up or installing an update | Wait, and don't switch the router off. |
| Solid amber | Connected to the fibre box but not yet to the internet | Wait 10 minutes, then restart the router. |
| Solid red | No signal from the Kestrel network | Check both ends of the network cable and restart the router. If the light stays red, report a fault. |
| No light | No power | Check the power supply and the socket. |

To restart the router, switch it off at the back, wait 30 seconds and switch it on again.

To report a fault, call technical support on 01632 960150 at any time, or report it online at www.kestrel.example/help.
