# Refund rules

Audience: customer
Status: current

This page explains when Kestrel refunds money to you and how long a refund takes.

## Cancelling in the cooling-off period

You can cancel a new order within 14 days of the day you placed it. This is the cooling-off period. If you cancel in the cooling-off period, you don't pay an early termination charge, and we refund anything you've already paid within 14 days of the day you cancel. Please return the router in the prepaid bag that we send you.

## Credit balances on closed accounts

When your account closes, we send a final bill. If the final bill shows a credit balance, we refund it automatically to the original payment method within 10 working days of the final bill.

## Credit balances on open accounts

A credit balance on an open account is normally taken off your next bill. If the balance is more than £10.00, you can ask billing to pay it back to you instead. We refund it to the original payment method within 10 working days of your request.

## Compensation and goodwill credits

Automatic compensation and goodwill credits are added to your bill as credits. They aren't paid as cash unless your account has closed, in which case they form part of your final balance.

## Disputed charges

If you think a charge on your bill is wrong, contact billing on 01632 960170. We review disputed charges within 5 working days. If we made a mistake, we credit the amount to your next bill, or refund it if you ask and the amount is more than £10.00.
