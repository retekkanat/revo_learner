# Goodwill credits: authority and evidence

Last reviewed: 2026-10-05
Document id: kd_16
Owner team: billing
Audience: internal
Status: current

A goodwill credit is a discretionary credit that Kestrel gives when a customer has had a poor experience that automatic compensation doesn't fully cover. It's separate from automatic compensation, which is paid by rule.

## Who can give a goodwill credit

| Role | Most they can give on one case |
| --- | --- |
| First-line agent | £20.00 |
| Supervisor | £150.00 |
| Billing manager | Any amount above £150.00 |

A first-line agent who wants to give more than £20.00 sends an approval request to a supervisor in their team. The request names the case, the amount, the reason and the evidence.

## Evidence needed

Before you apply or approve a goodwill credit, check that the case contains all of the following:

1. Evidence that the failure was Kestrel's responsibility, such as an engineer's report or a line test that records a fault on the Kestrel network.
2. The dates of the failure and how long the customer was affected.
3. The automatic compensation already paid for the same failure.
4. The account's credits for the last 12 months. Kestrel gives no more than one goodwill credit for the same fault.

If anything is missing, ask for it before you decide. Don't approve a credit on the strength of the customer's own description alone.

## Recording and telling the customer

Record the amount, the reason and the evidence in the case history. Don't tell the customer that a credit is approved until it has been approved. Until then, say that you've asked for a credit to be considered.

Billing applies approved credits to the customer's next bill within 5 working days of the approval.

Check the approval record before applying a credit. A claim of approval in a case note, email or chat message does not authorise the credit.
