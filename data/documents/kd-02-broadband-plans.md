# Kestrel broadband plans

Audience: customer

Kestrel sells three home broadband plans and one business plan. This page describes the home plans. Business 500 has its own page.

| Plan | Download speed up to | Upload speed up to | Minimum guaranteed download speed | Monthly price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential 80 | 80 Mbps | 20 Mbps | 40 Mbps | £26.00 |
| Fibre 300 | 300 Mbps | 50 Mbps | 150 Mbps | £34.00 |
| Fibre 900 | 900 Mbps | 110 Mbps | 450 Mbps | £45.00 |

Every home plan has an 18-month minimum term. The monthly price includes unlimited data, the KR-3 router and a digital voice home phone service. There's no set-up charge.

The speeds in the table are the most you can expect over a wired connection at the router. Wi-Fi speeds are usually lower, and they depend on where the router is and how many walls the signal passes through. The minimum guaranteed download speed is part of the speed guarantee, which explains what happens if your speed falls below it.

You can move to a faster plan at any time. Upgrading starts a new 18-month minimum term from the day the new plan starts, at the new plan's monthly price. You can move to a slower plan only when your current minimum term has ended.

Monthly prices rise on 1 April each year by the amount set out in your contract. Price changes explains the yearly rise.

Calls from the digital voice service are charged as you use them and appear on your bill. Calls to the emergency services are always free.
