# Making a complaint

Document id: kd_17
Owner team: customer_care

Status: current

If you're unhappy with Kestrel's service, tell us and we'll try to put it right. You can complain by calling customer care on 01632 960100, by emailing help@kestrel.example, or by using the form at www.kestrel.example/help.

## What happens after you complain

1. We acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days and tell you who is handling it.
2. We aim to resolve most complaints within 10 working days.
3. If we can't resolve your complaint quickly, a senior complaints handler takes it over and keeps you updated.
4. We send you a final response in writing within 8 weeks of the day you complained. It explains what we found, what we've done and anything we're offering to put things right.

## If you're still unhappy

If you aren't satisfied with our final response, or if 8 weeks have passed since you complained and we haven't sent one, you can refer your complaint to the independent dispute resolution scheme that Kestrel belongs to. The scheme is free for you to use. Our final response letter gives its contact details.

The scheme can ask Kestrel to apologise, to put something right or to pay compensation. Kestrel follows the scheme's decision.

## Help with making a complaint

If you need help to make a complaint, for example because of a disability, tell us and we'll make the process easier for you. You can also ask someone you trust to complain for you, once you've told us that they can act on your behalf.
