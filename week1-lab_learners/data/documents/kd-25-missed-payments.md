# Missed payments

Last reviewed: 2026-05-25
Document id: kd_25
Owner team: billing
Audience: customer
Status: current

If a payment fails, Kestrel tells you straight away and gives you time to put it right before any charge or restriction applies. Your bill's due date is the day we collect your direct debit, which is 10 days after the bill date.

## If your direct debit fails

We send you a text message and an email on the day the direct debit fails, and we try to collect the payment again 5 days later. If the second attempt also fails, pay by debit or credit card online or by calling billing on 01632 960170, and check the bank details in your online account.

## Late payment fee

If a bill is still unpaid 14 days after its due date, we add a late payment fee of £7.50 to your next bill. If your bank confirms that the payment failed because of its own error, send us the bank's confirmation and we'll remove the fee.

## If a bill stays unpaid

If a bill is still unpaid 28 days after its due date, we suspend your broadband until you pay. Calls to the emergency services from your digital voice home phone still work while your broadband is suspended. We restore your service within 4 hours of receiving payment.

## Payment plans

If you're finding it hard to pay, call billing on 01632 960170. We can spread an overdue balance over up to 3 months. We don't add late payment fees while you keep to the payment plan.
