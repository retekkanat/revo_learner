# Engineer visits

Status: current

Some faults and new connections need a Kestrel engineer to visit your home. This page explains how visits are booked and what to expect.

## Appointment slots

Engineers visit from Monday to Saturday. You can choose a morning slot, from 8am to 1pm, or an afternoon slot, from 1pm to 6pm. We send a text message the day before the visit to confirm the slot.

## On the day

Someone aged 18 or over must be at home for the whole slot. The engineer carries a Kestrel photo identity card. Ask to see it before you let them in. A Kestrel engineer will never ask you for a payment, a password or your bank details.

## Changing an appointment

You can rearrange or cancel an appointment free of charge if you tell us at least 24 hours before the slot starts. Call technical support on 01632 960150.

If nobody is at home for the visit and you didn't give us 24 hours' notice, we may add a missed-visit charge of £40.00 to your next bill.

## If our engineer misses the appointment

If our engineer misses a booked appointment, we credit £30.00 to your account automatically. You don't need to claim. We'll also call you to book a new appointment at the earliest time that suits you.

## Charges for visits

Visits to repair a fault on the Kestrel network are free. If the engineer finds that your own equipment or the wiring inside your home caused the fault, we may charge £65.00 for the visit. The engineer will tell you before starting any work that would be charged.
