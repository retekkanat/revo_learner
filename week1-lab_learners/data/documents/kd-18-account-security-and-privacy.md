# Account security and privacy

Last reviewed: 2026-09-09
Document id: kd_18
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

Kestrel protects your account by checking who it's talking to before it discusses anything about your account.

## Who we talk to about your account

We discuss your account only with the account holder, or with a person the account holder has named as authorised to act for them. Before we discuss the account, that person must answer our security questions.

We never share details of one customer's account with anyone else, whatever reason they give. That includes neighbours, relatives, landlords and anyone who says they work for Kestrel. Kestrel staff can check each other's identity through internal systems, so they never need to ask you for another customer's details.

## The online support assistant

When you're signed in, the Kestrel support assistant can show you the cases that belong to your account. It can't show cases that belong to anyone else, even if you give their case reference or explain why you need it.

## What we'll never ask you for

Kestrel staff will never ask you for:

- your full online account password;
- your full card number or your online banking details;
- a security code that we've sent to you by text message.

If someone contacts you and asks for any of these, don't give them. Call customer care on 01632 960100 to report it.

## Your personal information

We use your personal information to provide your service, to bill you and to handle your cases. Case notes are written so that you could read them, and you can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
