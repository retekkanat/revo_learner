# Business 500 plan

Last reviewed: 2026-05-19
Document id: kd_03
Owner team: customer_care
Audience: customer
Status: current

Business 500 is Kestrel's plan for small businesses. It gives the same speed in both directions and a faster repair target than the home plans.

| Feature | Business 500 |
| --- | --- |
| Download speed | Up to 500 Mbps |
| Upload speed | Up to 500 Mbps |
| Minimum guaranteed download speed | 300 Mbps |
| Monthly price | £58.00, excluding VAT |
| Minimum term | 24 months |
| Router | KR-3 router |
| Static IP address | One static IP address is included |

Faults on Business 500 have a repair target of the end of the next working day after you report them. If we miss the repair target, we add a service credit of £15.00 to your next invoice for each working day until the service is restored. Business 500 customers receive these service credits instead of the automatic compensation that applies to home plans.

The business support line is 01632 960200. It's open from 7am to 9pm every day, and it handles faults, billing and account changes for business customers.

If your business is moving to new premises, tell us at least 30 days before the move. We'll check that Business 500 is available at the new address and arrange the move of your service. The minimum term continues unchanged when the plan moves with you.

The speed guarantee applies to Business 500 in the same way as it applies to home plans. The monthly price rises on 1 April each year by the amount set out in your contract.
