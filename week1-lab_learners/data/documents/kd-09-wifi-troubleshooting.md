# Improving your Wi-Fi

Last reviewed: 2026-05-06
Document id: kd_09
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: current

Most slow-connection problems at home are Wi-Fi problems rather than faults on the line. These steps help you find out which kind of problem you have and improve your Wi-Fi.

## Find out whether the problem is Wi-Fi or the line

Connect a computer to the router with a network cable and run the Kestrel speed test at www.kestrel.example/speed. If the wired speed is normal for your plan, the line is working and the problem is with Wi-Fi. If the wired speed is also slow, follow the steps in Reporting a fault.

## Improve the Wi-Fi signal

- Put the router in a central place, off the floor and out of cupboards.
- Keep the router away from microwave ovens, cordless phone bases, fish tanks and large metal objects.
- Restart the router once to clear any temporary problem.
- If many neighbouring networks are nearby, change the Wi-Fi channel in the Kestrel app. The router chooses a channel automatically, but a manual choice sometimes works better in flats and terraced houses.

## Wi-Fi extenders

If the signal is still weak in some rooms after you've tried these steps, a Wi-Fi extender can help. Kestrel sells one for £60.00. Fibre 900 customers can ask for one free of charge.

Wi-Fi speeds don't count towards the speed guarantee. Only wired speed tests at the router count.
