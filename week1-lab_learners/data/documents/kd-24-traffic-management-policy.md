# Traffic management policy

Last reviewed: 2022-03-15
Document id: kd_24
Owner team: technical_support
Audience: customer
Status: superseded

This policy explains how Kestrel manages traffic on the Standard 38 and Superfast 76 plans at busy times, so that all customers get a fair share of the network.

## When traffic management applies

Traffic management applies between 6pm and 11pm every day. During these hours, if you've downloaded more than 250 GB since the start of the calendar month, we may slow peer-to-peer file sharing and large downloads on your connection until midnight. Web browsing, email, video calls and online gaming aren't slowed.

## How much your connection is slowed

When traffic management applies, peer-to-peer and large download traffic is limited to half of your plan's maximum download speed. Standard 38 customers are limited to 19 Mbps, and Superfast 76 customers are limited to 38 Mbps.

## Checking your usage

You can check how much you've downloaded this month in your online account. We send you an email when you reach 200 GB, so that you know that traffic management may start soon.

## Questions about this policy

If you have a question about traffic management, call our support line on 01632 960900 between 9am and 6pm, Monday to Friday.
